## Caveat

Ordered by a Wikipedia written by enthusiasts rather than by a corpus of Latin as it was used, so "commonest" here means commonest on that wiki. Validated against en.wiktionary lemmas only, so an inflected form is refused: AMARE plays and AMAVERUNT does not. The weakest thing about this list, and the same weakness Finnish has.
