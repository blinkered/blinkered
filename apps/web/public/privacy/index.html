Blinkered Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

Blinkered is a word game published by Tight Line LLC. This policy describes what the game collects, why, and what happens to it. It is short because the game collects little.

What we do not do

  • There is no advertising, and no data is sold or shared for advertising.
  • There are no analytics, no tracking pixels, and no third-party scripts of any kind on the site.
  • There is no profiling and no automated decision-making about you.

Playing without an account

You can play without signing in, and if you do, nothing about your game reaches us. Your settings and your recent scores are stored by your own browser, on your own device, and we cannot read them.

If you create an account

We store:

  • How you sign in. Your email address, and, if you use Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, the identifier that provider gives us for you and the email address it reports. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only ever see the forwarding address Apple creates.
  • Your profile. A username, and optionally a country, a short bio, and your language choices. Your avatar is drawn from your account identifier; there is nothing to upload and no picture of you.
  • Your games. For each finished game you keep: the settings it was played with, the score, and the words you found on the boards you found them on.
  • Sessions. A record that you are signed in, so you do not have to sign in again. We store a hash of the session token rather than the token.

Sign-in codes are stored hashed and expire. Nothing here includes your name, your address, your phone number, or any payment details, because the game never asks for them.

What is public

Profiles and finished games are public. Anyone with the link can see a player's username, avatar, country, bio, and the games they have kept, including the words found in them. If you do not want something to be public, do not put it in your username or bio.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in. Two more exist for a few minutes while you are signing in with Apple or Google, to make sure the sign-in that comes back is the one you started. That is all of them. There are no analytics or advertising cookies, so there is nothing to consent to and no banner asking you to.

Who else is involved

  • Apple and Google, only if you choose to sign in with them, and only to confirm who you are.
  • Google Workspace, which sends your sign-in codes by email.
  • Cloudflare, which serves playblinkered.com and sees the ordinary technical details of a web request, including your IP address.

Servers are operated by Tight Line LLC in the United States. If you are reading this in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, your information is transferred there.

How long we keep it

Your account and its games remain until you ask us to delete them. Sign-in codes expire within minutes. Sessions expire after thirty days of not being used.

Deleting your account

Write to help@playblinkered.com from the address on your account and we will delete it, along with your profile and your games. There is no button for this in the game yet; there will be.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of what we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. Write to the address above and we will do it. We will not charge you and we will not make you justify the request.

Children

Blinkered is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. If you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes and the new version replaces this one at this address.

Contact
